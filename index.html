How to read this

  • Source: Patent Bear full-text index of US and EP grants and published applications, filed from 1 Jan 2015, searched 15 Oct 2026. One search per theme (search string shown in the table).
  • Counts are publication records, not families: an application and its grant count twice. Use them for direction and relative size, not as family counts.
  • China (CN), Japan (JP) and Korea (KR) national filings are not covered. They dominate zeolite filing volume, so absolute counts understate the field — especially for detergent, MTO and commodity sieves.
  • Growth compares 2021–25 with 2016–20 publications. Small themes (<50 records) swing on a handful of filings.
  • “Signal” is the strategy team’s judgement against the plant-fill charter, not a patent metric.
  • Oxygen and hydrogen PSA could not be searched (monthly quota reached); both use LTA/X frameworks Zeolyst does not make.
  • “Pilot first” on PFAS reflects the evidence check: all zeolite PFAS data are lab scale, and pilots favour single-use resin.
  • The Orbit section below adds 1,689 live families from two Questel Orbit exports (15 Oct 2026), the only source here with Chinese, Japanese and Korean filings. 411 are not zeolite-based and are left out of its counts.
